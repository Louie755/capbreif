Why I built this

I played competitive hockey for twelve years, then moved to the other side of the glass: building analytics at NHL Analytica and breaking down the game on Ice Analytics.

The part nobody teaches well is the business — cap math, media rights, why a team takes on a contract it doesn't love. I wanted one place to read a deal, pull out the numbers that matter and actually remember them. So I made it.
