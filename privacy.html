Privacy

Last updated October 11, 2026

CapBrief is run by Louie Suh. This page explains what happens to your information.

What you send

Text, files and screenshots you submit are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to create your notes and answer your questions. We do not sell your content or use it for advertising.

What we keep

We keep a count of free notes per IP address for the current day so the free limit works. If you subscribe, we store your Stripe customer ID in a cookie to confirm your plan. Your saved library stays in your own browser and never reaches our server.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.

Your choices

Clear your browser storage to remove your library. Email us to cancel or to ask what we hold about you.

Questions? Email hello@capbrief.com.
